Claude Light · ESP32-S3 · WS2812 on GPIO48

What the light is telling you

A small LED on the desk that lights up when Claude Code is waiting on you. The colour says why it's waiting. The animation says how long it's been.

Colour: why Claude is waiting

Answer it and the light goes off.

Purple: a question

Claude asked you something and needs an answer to carry on.

clears when you answer

Amber: permission

Claude wants to run a command or edit a file and needs your OK.

clears when you allow or deny

Blue: finished

Claude is done with its turn and is waiting for your next message.

clears when you reply

Animation: how long it's been waiting

Every colour escalates the same way. Leave it long enough and it turns red, whatever the reason.

0 – 1 min

Calm

Dim, slow breathing. One breath every 4 seconds.

1 – 5 min

Nudging

Brighter, quicker breathing, about every 1.5 seconds.

5 – 15 min

Agitated

Bright, sharp pulses, twice a second.

15 min +

Frantic

Full-brightness strobe, drifting to red over the next 5 minutes.

Other things you might see

The light always shows the session that has been waiting longest.

Off

Nothing is waiting on you. Claude is working, or idle with nothing to ask.

Double-blink

Two or more sessions are waiting. A quick double flash every 4 seconds.

Faint white blip

The light has lost Wi-Fi and is reconnecting. One blip every 3 seconds.

Animations here are slowed for the screen. The real Frantic strobe is faster. Switch it off: create ~/.claude/claude-light.off Sessions forgotten after 2 hours
